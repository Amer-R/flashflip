- (requirement) R1 (brief Solution 1): pasting term - definition lines makes one card per line containing ' - ', split at the first ' - '; the page shows how many lines were skipped
- (requirement) R2 (brief Solution 2): cards are shuffled at the start of every round
- (requirement) R3 (brief Solution 2): cards appear one at a time showing the term; tapping reveals the definition
- (requirement) R4 (brief Solution 2): the student marks each card Knew it or Missed it and moves to the next
- (requirement) R5 (brief Solution 3): at the end of a round the student sees a score of cards known out of cards in the round, e.g. 7 / 10
- (requirement) R6 (brief Solution 3): after the score the student can start a round with only the missed cards
- (requirement) R7 (brief Solution): the pasted deck survives a page reload; Knew it / Missed it results last only for the current visit
- (requirement) R8 (brief Solution): phone-first layout, one card per screen, large tap targets; also works on a laptop
