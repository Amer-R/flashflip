## 2026-11-10 — Inputs and stated decisions

Stated by the user in the invocation. Treated as decided until challenged.

- **Idea, verbatim:** "FlashFlip: a one-page web app that helps first-year university students revise. They paste lines like "term - definition" copied from their own notes, flip through cards, mark each card "knew it" or "missed it", and see their score. Students will paste messy text, use phones, and write in different languages. No login, data saved in the browser. Keep it an MVP."
- **Audience and use:** first-year university students, revision.
- **Input:** pasted plain text, lines like "term - definition", copied from the student's own notes; may be messy.
- **Core loop:** flip a card, mark it "knew it" or "missed it", see the score.
- **Context of use:** phones are a primary device; card content comes in different languages.
- **Constraints:** one page, no login, data saved in the browser.
- **Scope:** MVP.

## 2026-11-10 — Draft v1 assumptions

Proposed by the facilitator, unconfirmed until the user replies.

1. The 20-minute budget is for building the MVP, not only for this brief. (Withdrawn: see "User correction" below.)
2. Problem framing: notes are not self-testing material, and flashcard tools add retyping, setup and accounts.
3. Differentiation is friction only, with no moat. To be checked against the landscape digest (still pending).
4. Parser: split each line at its first tab, spaced dash (-, –, —) or colon; strip bullets, numbers and extra spaces; ignore blank lines; list lines with no separator.
5. A dash counts as a separator only when it has spaces around it, so hyphenated words survive.
6. Interface in English; card text in any language, including right-to-left and CJK.
7. One deck at a time; a new paste replaces it; deck and progress survive a reload; a visible "saved on this device only" note.
8. "Missed it" only counts toward the score in the MVP. Retry-missed and shuffle are stretch.
9. Cut for the budget: accounts, sync, multiple decks, editing, spaced repetition, file import and export, sharing, translated interface, media, stats, offline install.
10. Success thresholds (30 seconds to first card; a ~20-line messy paste) are placeholders.
