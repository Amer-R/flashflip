## 2026-11-10 — Spec decisions made in the draft (unconfirmed)

1. Precision over recall: a Line is listed as a Skipped line rather than guessed at (FR-4, FR-5). This gives up forgiving sloppy Separators such as `term- definition`.
2. Separator precedence: tab, then a spaced dash run, then a colon followed by whitespace or a fullwidth colon, then "——" (FR-4).
3. One leading list marker is removed: bullets and one-to-three-digit numbers, not letters (FR-3).
4. A Ready screen sits between "Make cards" and "Start" to show Skipped lines, a 3-Card preview and the Shuffle setting (FR-7). This adds a tap in exchange for visibility into mis-splits.
5. "Knew it" and "Missed it" stay disabled until the Card is flipped, and there is no undo (FR-9).
6. Shuffle is off by default, changeable only between Passes, with the order fixed at Pass start (FR-12), and tested for uniformity (FR-13).
7. Limits are 200,000 characters and 1,000 Cards (FR-6).
8. Duplicate Lines make duplicate Cards (FR-5).
9. Saved state stays in the MVP from the brief, though the instruction did not list it. Paste text typed before "Make cards" is not saved (FR-14, §7.2).
10. Retry-missed stays out of the MVP, flagged with a [NOTE FOR PM] (§7.2).
